What is Dungeons of Eternity?

Dungeons of Eternity is a virtual reality game where you can go into dungeons with randomly generated rooms and fight hordes of monsters with a variety of weapons that also come with a variety of elements and additional perks. There is also additional customization with the exosuit feature that allows the assigment of one perk for the mind, body, arms, and legs.

Where to start?

I think a good place to start would be going over simpler things like elements, and exosuit perks

Elements

Fire
Fire is a solid element, it creates an explosion when the element activates randomly and it can send enemies that are not bosses flying.
Ice
Ice is by far the best element in the game, it freezes an enemy that is not a boss or an ice elemental and kills the enemy no matter what hits it next. This means you would probably be fine on the hardest difficulty dungeons with a low level ice weapon.
Poison
Poison is a misleading title because as far as I know, it does not work the same way poison works traditionally, with it doing damage over time and maybe reducing speed or attack, poison in this game just stuns the enemy until they get hit. This element is pretty useful because it works on all enemy types, even poison enemies and bosses.

Exosuit perks

This section will be short too becasuse there is a grand total of five perks even worth using.

Head
They all suck, but I use mystify. It gives two enemies in the entire game a 50% to miss their projectiles: The archer who does a whopping 7 damage out of 100 and the mage who does 25 per hit, but i would be more worried about getting close because on higher difficulties, it can kill you almost instantly doing 75 damage with melee attacks.
Body
This category has the most perks, but there is only one worth using, vitality, it autoheals and funnily enough when maxed out outpunches archers.
Arms
This is the important one, as it will change based on the weapons you have.
  • Power: when maxed, it increases melee damage by 50%
  • Critical: when maxed, gives a 40% chance to increase melee damage by 200%
  • Pullback: when maxed, increases arrow/bolt damage by 50%
  • Impale: When maxed, gives a 40% chance to increase arrow/bolt damage by 200%
  • Might: When maxed, increases damage of thrown weapons by 50%
  • Gemini: Makes power gems last longer in staves.
Legs

Side Weapons

Side weapons are also important to your build, and determine what you can do when not using your back weapon. There are four categories of side weapons.

Swords

Swords are generally pretty good for most things, but unlike the next types are not throwable unless they have the throwable perk.

There are also scimitars that deal higher damage, but I don't think they can do stabbing damage.

Axes

Axes are great. You can swing them like swords while also being throwable without a perk meaning you can have other perks taking those slots. Axes can also be held in both hands making them do more damage.

I'm pretty sure sickles are just axes that look different.

Hammers, Maces, Bludgeons

They're all the same thing with a different look. These weapons are unique in that they are the only side weapons that have the slowing perk, are throwable, and are effective against armored enemies.

Daggers

I don't like daggers personally because of their low damage and the fact that they are small making them hard to parry with, but daggers are good for throwing as they come back the fastest and have exclusive access to the poison perk, which is just the poison element.
