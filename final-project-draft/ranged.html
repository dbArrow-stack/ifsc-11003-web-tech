====Ranged====

The Ranged class covers the back weapons: Bow and crossbow. This might get long, so here's a tldr, I wont be mad if you click it... I'm not reading all that.

Bow
The bow is pretty simple, it just requires aim. Or at least it did before the mythic bow hit the game. This is the back weapon i've used the most, and it kinda sucks that the skills i have developed with this weapon are irrelevant once level 60 is reached. I talk more about mythic weapons here. -> Mythic weapons
Crossbow
The crossbow is a funny weapon. It is useless without the perk reload. Reload allows you to fire more bolts after nocking. A multishot crossbow with the ice element is probably one of if not the best weapon in the game.

Summary

Bows for power, crossbow(with reload!) for speed.
